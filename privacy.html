Privacy Policy

Application: Claude Workspace MCP - Personal
Effective: October 1, 2026

Who uses this app

Only the owner of this Google Cloud project, with Google accounts the owner controls. It is not a public service.

What data is accessed

When the owner authorizes it, the app can access data in the owner's Google account for the services the owner approved on Google's consent screen: Gmail (read, label, archive, draft; no sending), Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Tasks, and Contacts (read-only).

How data is used

Data is accessed only to carry out tasks the owner requests through their own Claude Desktop application. It is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

The app runs locally on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are stored in a folder on that computer that is readable only by the owner's Windows account. The app operator runs no servers and keeps no copies of Google user data.

Content the owner asks Claude to work with is processed by Anthropic's Claude service under the owner's own Anthropic account and Anthropic's terms.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, and local tokens can be deleted from the owner's computer.
